On Windows, Radiate installs a copy of mpv next to itself, as a separate
program (radiate-mpv). Radiate starts it to play channels, the way open-tv
does. Radiate itself is MIT licensed; mpv is not part of it. When mpv is
already installed on the computer, Radiate uses that one instead.
